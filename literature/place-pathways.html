Place, pathways and regional cost context

Public climate source areas · two pathways · explicit geography and model coverage · 22 October 2026

Read the source area before the number

The explorer accounts for all 575 Nations: 513 associated, 34 explicitly labeled proxy contexts and 28 without a supported current source-area association. It publishes 526 distinct Native Resilience source areas and 4 separate USGS county/watershed contexts. Shared source areas are counted once in the climate table; no Nation-weighted national climate mean is inferred.

These areas support climate interpretation; they are not comprehensive sovereign territories. A proxy is neighboring or regional context, not a validated Nation mean. Several Nations may share a source area; Navajo agency areas remain separate choices. Geometry membership uses the documented public source-area intersection, not political region membership.

Native Resilience uses annual NASA NEX-GDDP-CMIP6 member values at approximately 25 km source resolution. Each model is compared with its own 1995-2014 baseline before equal-model ensemble summaries are calculated. Values below are 20-year mean changes; brackets show the 10th-90th percentile of model changes, not confidence intervals or scenario probabilities.

SSP2-4.5 and SSP3-7.0 are conditional pathways. Differences need not be monotonic at an individual place or time window. Temperature is degrees Celsius; precipitation is change in mean annual total, millimetres per year. Source annual tables do not retain realization IDs, calendar names or valid daily cell coverage.

The three story footprints provide regional context. Each example below uses one explicitly named climate source area selected by the explorer; it is not a climate average over the story footprint.

County cost context for the three story footprints

Whole-county Expected Annual Loss covers all county assets and residents. It is an annualized model expectation, not Tribal loss, projected climate-change damage or funding need. Each county is counted once within a footprint; footprints can overlap and their totals must not be added. Missing fields remain unavailable.

FEMA National Risk Index December 2025 v1.20. The climate-sensitive subtotal uses the supported 15-hazard scope; it excludes earthquake, tsunami and volcano. A common price year is not supplied by the cached input. The companion CSV retains each hazard, county coverage and unmatched county IDs.

Public modeled land-area geometry is not a comprehensive statement of Nation territory, ownership or sovereign authority. Association carries a legacy reviewed name/code crosswalk forward to current source IDs; it is not independent Nation confirmation. Shared and borrowed areas remain explicit. EPA/CEC 2010 taxonomy does not cover Hawaii; the downloaded feature table has 182 Level III units; older linked metadata says 181. Geographic memberships may overlap. Deduplicate Nation IDs, source areas and county FIPS separately. County context includes assets outside Tribal lands and is not a future Tribal loss forecast. Display geometry is simplified separately; all intersections use unsimplified source geometry. Intersections at or below 1 square metre are numerical slivers and excluded. EPA/CEC boundaries are broad-scale ecological context at 1:250,000 source scale; small intersections do not establish locally precise ecological boundaries. Associated land areas carry forward the pinned crosswalk; the association is not an exhaustive inventory of each Nation’s lands.

Frozen public-source summaries. No participant responses are collected. Use the linked source records and consult the relevant Nation before interpreting planning coverage.
